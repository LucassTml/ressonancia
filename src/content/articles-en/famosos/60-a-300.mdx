## Tuning as a decision about use

The Shure SE215 and the Sennheiser IE 40 Pro were born for the stage, and the curve shows it: boosted bass in one case, recessed presence in the other, choices that help a musician hear themselves at high volume without fatigue. The Truthear Nova, on the other hand, aims at the target with precision, and has the smallest difference from Harman in the whole gallery between 100 Hz and 5 kHz.

The 7Hz Timeless offers a different lesson. Its planar driver sounded like a novelty when it arrived, but what the curve mainly shows is excess energy above 10 kHz. Driver technology doesn't decide the tuning on its own; see [Planar magnetic driver](/drivers/planar-magnetico).

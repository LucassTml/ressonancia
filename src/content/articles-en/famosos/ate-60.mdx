## Two strategies, side by side

This range shows better than any other the difference between **specification** and **tuning**. On one side are models that advertise several drivers and impress in the first few minutes with elevated bass and treble. On the other are single-dynamic-driver models, simple and well tuned.

Compare the curve of the KZ ZS10, with five drivers per side, with that of the Moondrop Chu, with only one. The argument about how many drivers there are disappears once the two measurements appear together. The technical explanation of how a set of drivers has to be divided and tuned is in [Multi-driver and crossovers](/drivers/multi-driver-e-crossovers).

## Three schools

The **Etymotic** line follows an old idea: the diffuse-field target, with no Harman bass boost, deep insertion and very high isolation. The ER4SR sits 10 dB below Harman in the sub-bass and its XR version recovers about 4 dB. If you like the idea it sounds exact; if you don't, it sounds thin. [Targets and the Harman curve](/resposta/targets-e-harman) helps explain why.

**Moondrop** and its partners follow the preference school, with the Blessing 2 practically on target from 60 Hz to 18 kHz.

**Stage monitors**, such as the Shure SE535, have priorities of their own, and the result is dark and centered on the mids. It isn't a tuning mistake. It's another era and another use.

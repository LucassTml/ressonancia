## The seal comes before everything

An IEM only works properly if it seals the ear canal. Without a seal, the bass simply disappears, and the most expensive earphone in the world sounds thin and weak.

<Diagram id="seal" />

Try every eartip size in the box. Many people have different-sized canals in each ear. Play something with bass, fit the earphone and press lightly: if the bass grows a lot when you press, the seal was failing.

## What to look at when buying

1. **Tuning.** Look for independent measurements and compare them with a target. If the graph is close to Harman through the mids, the odds of liking it are good.
2. **Comfort and shape.** Large bodies don't fit every ear. Look for reports from people with ears like yours, or buy from shops with easy returns.
3. **Detachable cable.** The cable is the first thing to break, and being able to replace it extends the earphone's life.
4. **Isolation.** If you listen on the bus, it matters a lot, and it also helps you keep the volume lower.
5. **Microphone or Bluetooth,** if you need them. Wired IEMs can gain Bluetooth with adapters.

## Common mistakes

- **Counting drivers.** More drivers don't mean better sound. A five-driver IEM can be far worse tuned than a single-driver one; the [gallery](/famosos/ate-60) shows it with real measurements.
- **Trusting vague words.** "Detailed," "musical" and "warm" mean different things to different people. The graph helps you find out what someone is talking about.
- **Buying an amplifier first.** Almost no IEM needs one.
- **Judging in two minutes.** Always compare at the same volume, because the louder sound tends to seem better even when it isn't.

## How much to spend

Measurements show that getting the tuning right has stopped being expensive: there are IEMs at about US$ 20 very close to the target. Paying more buys build, accessories, comfort, brand and character, and that may well be worth it to you, but it isn't needed to hear music very well.

<Callout type="chave" title="A short plan">
Set a budget, pick two or three well-measured models in that range, get the eartip right when they arrive, and listen for a few weeks before deciding you need something else.
